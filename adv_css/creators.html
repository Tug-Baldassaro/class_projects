George R.R. Martin

George R.R. Martin is the author of the A Song of Ice and Fire series, which serves as the basis for the television show Game of Thrones. Martin is a prolific writer who has won numerous awards for his work, including the Hugo Award and the Nebula Award. His super detailed world building and complex characters have made the series a beloved favorite among fans around the world.His literary breakthrough arrived with the 1991 publication of A Game of Thrones, the first volume in his masterwork series. The books are celebrated for their gritty realism, complex political maneuvering, and a willingness to kill off major characters. This crazy narrative universe later served as the basis for HBOs blockbuster television.

David Benioff and D. B. Weiss

David Benioff and D. B. Weiss are an acclaimed American screenwriting and production duo best known for creating and running the HBO fantasy series Game of Thrones. The pair met in Dublin in 1995 while studying Irish literature and later transitioned into screenwriting, collaborating on various film scripts before tackling George R.R. Martin's book series. Their adaptation of Game of Thrones became a global cultural phenomenon, earning them multiple Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Drama Series and writing. Following the conclusion of the series in 2019, they signed a high-profile, multi-year overall deal with Netflix to write and produce new television and film projects. Despite facing significant criticism for the final seasons of Game of Thrones, they remain among the most influential and prominent showrunners in modern television history.
